    History comes from the RSI Backtester's price cache, updated after each US market close. The page then asks Yahoo Finance for a newer price through a relay (the CORS proxy set in the Backtester's Settings, or the r.jina.ai reader when none is set), so the ticker you look up is sent to that relay. The answer is saved in this browser and reused for ten minutes, then checked again; if the relay fails, the newest saved answer is shown when it is newer than the site's close, otherwise the site's last close. The 1D chart's 5-minute prices are not saved. Tickers outside the cache are looked up on Yahoo in full. Prices are adjusted for splits and dividends, so older prices read lower than the quotes printed at the time, and returns include dividends. During market hours the latest point is the current price, not a close. RSI uses Wilder's smoothing on daily closes, the same method as RSI Signals and Composer. Not financial advice.
